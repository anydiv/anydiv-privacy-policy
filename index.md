ANYDIV PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: May 2026

Thank you for using AnyDiv.

AnyDiv is designed to help users manage and split shared expenses fairly and simply. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how information is handled while using the app.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

AnyDiv does not require account creation or user registration.

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information through external servers.

The app allows users to create groups, members, and expenses for personal expense management purposes.

All data entered into the app, including:

• Group names
• Member names
• Expenses
• Shared amounts
• Settlement information

is stored locally on your device.

2. LOCAL DATA STORAGE

AnyDiv stores app data locally using on-device storage.

Your expense data remains on your device and is not uploaded to external cloud servers by the app.

You are responsible for maintaining access to your own device and local app data.

3. SHARING FEATURES

AnyDiv provides optional sharing functionality that allows users to share expense summaries through supported applications such as messaging or email apps.

Sharing is always initiated manually by the user.

The app does not automatically send data to any person or service.

4. CURRENCY & REGION SETTINGS

AnyDiv may use your device’s regional settings to display currency formatting appropriate to your location.

No geographic location data is collected or stored.

5. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

AnyDiv is not specifically designed for children under 13 years of age.

6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

AnyDiv V1 does not use:

• User accounts
• Cloud storage
• Advertising services
• Analytics tools
• Payment processing
• Third-party tracking systems

Future versions may introduce optional features, and this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. DATA SECURITY

Since data is stored locally on your device, users are encouraged to protect their device using appropriate security measures.

8. ORGANIZER-FIRST APP MODEL

AnyDiv is designed primarily as a single-user organizer application where one person manages shared expenses on behalf of a group.

9. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Changes will be reflected in updated versions of the app and documentation.
